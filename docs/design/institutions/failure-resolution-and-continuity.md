# Failure, resolution, and continuity

**ID:** `design.institutions.failure-resolution-and-continuity`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.institutions.legal-regime`, `design.state.correction-and-invalidation`

Illiquidity, insolvency, payment failure, capital impairment, operational outage, lost confidence, political coercion, and procedural breakdown remain distinct canonical conditions. There is no universal failure meter. A condition changes authority or control only through the applicable legal, contractual, or operational transition.

A `ResolutionProceeding` is a record in the custody of the responsible authority. Its statutory transitions may witness receivership, conservatorship, control changes, bridge arrangements, stays, transfers, recapitalization, or wind-down. The proceeding does not absorb the affected property, accounts, contracts, or authority. Case files and situation views cannot transfer title or legal control.

Failure usually creates a new regime and altered action set before it creates a terminal screen. Emergency law, imposed coordination, revolt, diminished independence, or failed intervention may constrain the Chair while play continues. Official records, facilities, commitments, proceedings, staff, legal state, credibility consequences, and damaged capacity persist across officeholders and scenario transitions.

A repair, reversal, or recovery is a new witnessed path. It cannot erase incurred costs, failed attempts, breached promises, or historical ownership. Campaign termination follows the bounded campaign rule rather than an arbitrary mechanism failure.
