# Staff capability and condition

**ID:** `design.institutions.staff-capability-and-condition`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.state.cognition-beliefs-and-plans`, `design.state.agreements-commitments-and-capacity`

Federal Reserve staff are institutionally competent. Variation separates four systems. `Capability` records bounded professional expertise and may provide a clear domain advantage. `Disposition` records judgment, salience, and source-weighting tendencies whose value depends on context. `Condition` records temporary workload, fatigue, exclusion, stale inputs, access limits, scrutiny, or disruption. `Presentation` supplies authored labels and rendering without changing mechanics.

Staff units do not acquire one institutional mind. An assessment persists model output, analyst judgment, source evidence, unit synthesis, dissent, conditional branches, channel hypotheses, feasibility, confidence, omissions, and unmodeled uncertainty. Contributors retain their own information and beliefs. The record's author and custody remain explicit.

Tasks reserve dated people, team, access, review, and delivery capacity. Over-requesting creates visible delay, displacement, narrower evidence, or weaker confidence rather than a universal mana penalty. Ordinary permitted access is obtained through competent workflow; consequential conflicts surface as bounded options with timing and opportunity costs.

Staff can disagree, misweight evidence, miss connections, or be wrong without general incompetence. No trait grants canonical truth, inaccessible evidence, legal authority, or guaranteed outcomes.
