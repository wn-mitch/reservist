# Real-economy network

**ID:** `design.economy.real-economy-network`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.economy.accounting-and-settlement`, `design.representation.populations-households-and-lenses`

Real stocks and flows are coequal with financial claims and obligations. The model sits between a small aggregate sector system and a full input-output economy. Household and business cohorts carry income, employment, debt service, financing, consumption, prices, capacity, inventories, and material exposure. Selected strategic firms and productive sites receive richer state only where their distinct actions change a channel.

Closed product-family types define stage, grade, unit, storage, transformation, transport, substitution, spoilage or loss, and settlement-relevant attributes. Production and transformation consume owned inputs and capacity, create owned outputs and losses, and emit witnesses. Industry cohorts and product flows connect upstream resources and intermediate goods to downstream firms and household budgets.

Production follows one staged grammar without one universal response equation.
A right holder proposes dated work; effective law, rights, quotas, contracts,
and eligibility authorize or limit it; an operating mechanism admits work
against equipment and input constraints; execution consumes recoverable state
or titled inputs; witnesses record outputs, co-products, grade, loss, waste,
custody, and provenance; accepted output enters its title-bearing accounts.
Capacity distinguishes nameplate, technically available, legally available,
input-supported, committed, and realized throughput.

Commodity extensions may add depletion, recovery, joint output, biological or
harvest timing, restart delay, grade distributions, substitution, storage, and
network constraints. Petroleum, gas, LNG, refining, water treatment, power,
petrochemicals, fertilizer, freight, and other industries use this grammar only
through their own typed products, technologies, owners, and stage contracts.

Every resource system keeps policy authority and operations as separate
owners at every fidelity. Policy authority sets and commits posture, targets,
and terms. Operations realizes extraction, processing, and export availability
under actual capacity and inputs. An announcement or authorized target is not
realized output, and the policy owner cannot write barrels, inventories,
transport, delivery, or prices. Coarse fidelity may compress state and behavior
but may not merge owners that vary independently, hold different canonical
state, or exercise different authority.

Financial conditions affect real decisions through specific contracts, accounts, refinancing, demand, and constraints. Physical shortages affect prices, production, employment, and claims through typed transmissions. Neither side may become a generic modifier over the other.

Major physical development occurs only inside a reviewed feasible-project
envelope naming site or route, technology, eligible owners, capacity range,
cost and lead time, permissions, and physical dependencies. Authorized actors
may propose, finance, resize, delay, accelerate, mothball, expand, or cancel a
reachable project. Price or need cannot invent geography, technology, or an
unregistered project during play.

Long-tail entities use typed cohorts and residual reconciliation. Aggregate adapters expose bounded interfaces for omitted detail. They cannot create goods, title, capacity, or final prices without an owner-specific process and balanced state transition.
