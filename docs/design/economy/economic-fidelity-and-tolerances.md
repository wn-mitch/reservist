# Economic fidelity and tolerances

**ID:** `design.economy.economic-fidelity-and-tolerances`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.economy.opening-conditions-and-transmission`

Reservist is a decision-shaped, turn-based abstraction rather than a complete general-equilibrium reconstruction. Within-period mechanics may compress long processes while preserving evidence-backed direction, causal order, regime transitions, constraints, bottlenecks, and pivotal tails. Exact thresholds remain exact where law, contracts, accounting, or institutional procedure defines them.

Conservation and legal ownership are globally exact. Material stocks, behavior, timing, and distribution tails use separate channel-specific approximation budgets. Scenarios may protect concentrations, eligibility thresholds, authority boundaries, bottlenecks, and discontinuities that an aggregate error budget would erase. Approximation error is named and cannot be disguised as randomness.

Validation combines hard invariants, mechanism predicates, sibling fixtures, multi-seed distribution bands, and holdout observation. A mechanism need not reproduce one unknowable historical price path. It must permit the same class of mechanism under plausible conditions and distinguish materially different regimes through institutionally obtainable evidence.

Practical guidance explains what an instrument does, not which choice is correct. Holdout playtests test whether players inspect and use consequential distinctions rather than memorize one package. Numerical channel budgets, final coefficients, and exact score bands remain explicit calibration work.
