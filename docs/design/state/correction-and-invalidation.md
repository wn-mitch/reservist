# Correction and invalidation

**ID:** `design.state.correction-and-invalidation`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.state.actions-results-and-events`

Changing an upstream choice invalidates only affected drafts, derived projections, and unresolved future work. It never rewrites committed history. A cancellation, correction, amendment, reversal, supersession, legal change, or accounting repair is a new typed transition under the affected owner's lifecycle, with its own authority, timing, reason, and witness.

An open decision folder may recompute its prospective combined slate when penciled choices change. After handoff, the original folder, commands, receipts, commitments, and witnesses remain immutable. A still-pending proposal may be amended through a linked new handoff. A resolved institutional decision cannot be replaced by a later Chair proposal; the real legal or procedural lifecycle governs any change.

Derived evidence and retrospective judgments follow the same rule. Later evidence can revise confidence or produce a supplemental assessment, but it cannot silently edit what was known or concluded earlier. Accounting corrections use compensating entries. Score revisions use explicit signed adjustments. Presentation-only changes do not rerun mechanics.

Cross-phase feedback follows the owning runtime cadence. A correction that targets an earlier completed phase enters a later tick or period unless a mechanism explicitly owns a bounded clearing loop.
