# Agreements, commitments, and capacity

**ID:** `design.state.agreements-commitments-and-capacity`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.state.canonical-state-and-ownership`

An agreement is a durable relationship container whose clauses create specific permissions, duties, transfers, access, maintenance costs, breach consequences, and exit rules. Clauses remain independently effective unless their contract explicitly couples them. A relationship edge alone never transfers title, authority, access, or stock.

Every consequential action may create typed commitments over people, teams, money, balance sheet, authority, meeting windows, operational capacity, coalition support, political tolerance, credibility exposure, or promised communication. Commitments identify an owner, allocation, start, duration or release condition, expected deliverable, contingent calls, and any public or private proposition at risk.

Capacity is concrete and non-fungible. Staff time cannot silently substitute for legal approval; balance sheet cannot replace an available meeting; political tolerance cannot settle cash. A dated allocation remains occupied until completion, explicit cancellation, or another modeled release transition. Completion produces the declared output and witnesses release.

Any Leash or similar pressure display is a derived projection over commitments and conditions. It owns no reservation, is not conserved, and cannot independently gate an action. Practical cards expose exact immediate capacity use, expected completion, displaced alternatives, and release risks without predicting hidden outcomes.
