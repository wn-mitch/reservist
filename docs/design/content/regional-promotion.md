# Regional promotion

**ID:** `design.content.regional-promotion`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.promotion-fallback-and-residuals`, `design.content.relationships-and-transmissions`

A regional atlas is a planning roster of candidate scopes and channel interfaces, not a promise of uniform fidelity or scenario inclusion. A region remains non-owning. Internal institutions, firms, people, productive sites, accounts, processes, and providers own every stock, action, condition, and transition.

Promotion requires evidence that an independently varying condition or choice unique to a candidate changes a modeled actor's feasible actions, accessible observations, or settlement outcomes. Geography, historical importance, available prose, or player interest alone does not qualify. Promotion occurs before initialization from cataloged entries and researched state.

Each regional candidate declares the receiving Fed-facing channel, stable inputs and outputs, units, timing, product and grade scope, account scope, delivery windows, relationships, providers, residual share, fallback, and required probe. Rich representation replaces its declared aggregate share. It cannot layer upon an existing external adapter or create duplicate quantities.

Concrete regional identities, readiness, and period profiles live in catalog data. An atlas proposal makes no entry selectable and no scenario ready until all catalog, relationship, transmission, opening-state, provider, manifest, and probe contracts validate.
