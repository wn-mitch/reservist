# Scenario selection

**ID:** `design.runtime.scenario-selection`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.content.manifests-and-supported-transitions`, `design.representation.promotion-fallback-and-residuals`

Scenario initialization fixes the representation manifest, causal roster, owner classes, selected fidelity models, period variants, residual mappings, protected population dimensions, permitted transitions, fallbacks, and aggregation boundaries for the run. A generator may choose among predefined reviewed content before initialization; the runtime may not invent a richer causal representation because a subject becomes salient.

Every scenario separates an immutable historical prefix, the canonical opening snapshot effective at start, and a future queue of inherited obligations plus contingent events. The past remains fixed. Future law, appointments, removals, succession, commitments, and outcomes remain contingent inside campaign bounds.

A manifest selects catalog IDs and models and may override declared parameters, never structure. It must close over every action, legal transition, provider response, succession path, and fallback the scenario claims to support. Missing or incompatible bindings prevent start or resume. The manifest cannot arbitrarily whitelist or erase lawful actions; current effective authority and state decide runtime eligibility.

Presentation may foreground any initialized subject without changing causal resolution or frozen identity.
