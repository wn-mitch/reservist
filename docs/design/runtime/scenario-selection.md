# Scenario selection

**ID:** `design.runtime.scenario-selection`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.content.manifests-and-supported-transitions`, `design.representation.promotion-fallback-and-residuals`

Scenario initialization fixes the representation manifest, causal roster, owner classes, selected fidelity models, period variants, residual mappings, protected population dimensions, permitted transitions, fallbacks, and aggregation boundaries for the run. A generator may choose among predefined reviewed content before initialization; the runtime may not invent a richer causal representation because a subject becomes salient.

Every M4 world profile initializes every sovereign effective under its
source-backed dated roster. The roster declares recognition disputes and
changes; coarse fidelity never permits silent omission. Each sovereign has an
identity-only root and a closed typed coarse component bundle; the manifest may
replace declared component shares with researched rich owners. M1 and M2
preserve their frozen narrower external-adapter boundaries and do not
retroactively acquire this roster.

Every scenario separates an immutable historical prefix, the canonical opening
snapshot effective at start, and a future queue of inherited obligations,
recorded external occurrences, and contingent decisions. The past remains
fixed. A recorded occurrence is permitted only on an external channel with no
player-reachable feedback; its downstream outcome is not fixed. Future law,
appointments, removals, succession, commitments, responsive branches, and
economic outcomes remain contingent inside campaign bounds.

A manifest selects catalog IDs and models and may override declared parameters, never structure. It must close over every action, legal transition, provider response, succession path, and fallback the scenario claims to support. Missing or incompatible bindings prevent start or resume. The manifest cannot arbitrarily whitelist or erase lawful actions; current effective authority and state decide runtime eligibility.

Each external political channel declares `RECORDED` or `RESPONSIVE` before
initialization. The manifest closes recorded tapes, responsive state and
interventions, and every rich-to-coarse fallback. Exhausted rich content may
continue only by deterministic reconciliation into that selected compatible
coarse model. With no compatible model, selection fails or the run reaches an
authored terminal frontier; runtime approximation is forbidden.

Presentation may foreground any initialized subject without changing causal resolution or frozen identity.
