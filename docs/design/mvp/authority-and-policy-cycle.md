# Authority and policy cycle

**ID:** `design.mvp.authority-and-policy-cycle`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.institutions.authority-and-action-ownership`, `design.institutions.policy-portfolios`

The playable cycle carries evidence and inherited commitments into staff work, a bounded policy folder, FOMC procedure, authorization, directive execution, structured communication, intermeeting consequences, and the next Morning Book. The player may inspect, request work, compare packages, pencil choices, speak authored lines, submit a slate, advance the calendar, and review receipts and later evidence.

The Chair proposes and communicates but does not own FOMC votes. Represented participants retain their judgments. The FOMC procedure owns eligibility, agenda, voting, and the policy decision. The authorized directive bounds operating action; the responsible Reserve Bank executes. Each stage emits its own result and witness.

The scenario supports the three authored policy packages preserved by the executable baseline. Submission is not execution, and execution does not guarantee market or audience response. An unchosen default proposal never becomes policy merely because time advances.

The cycle ends only after selected intermeeting releases, staff revisions, audience interpretations, market effects, commitments, and outstanding work are reflected in the next bounded institutional briefing. It does not award historical correctness or extend into succession.
