# Population and media scope

**ID:** `design.mvp.population-and-media-scope`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.populations-households-and-lenses`, `design.information.media-circuit`

The MVP selects only the population and audience resolution needed to close the policy cycle. Canonical person cells and household cohorts carry the material exposure required for employment, housing, consumption, debt service, and attention. Non-owning lenses derive worker, homeowner, borrower, market-participant, and low-attention views without creating separate stocks or collective minds.

Communication uses structured claims and a bounded set of direct audience interpretations. Publication passes through selected institutional and media channels with source, framing, timing, credibility, and witnesses. Audiences may update beliefs differently from the same policy act and later alter bounded behavior. No runtime prose generation or universal mass-public score is present.

The scenario uses explicit delivery edges rather than a general social graph. Unselected outlets, commentators, organizations, and regional audiences remain outside the causal roster or inside declared residual providers. Presentation may mention only what authored records and selected evidence permit.

The closing Morning Book reports institutionally observed population, market, and media consequences with uncertainty. It does not expose canonical aggregate truth or claim that the initial cut proves broader population calibration, network dynamics, or transferable learning.
