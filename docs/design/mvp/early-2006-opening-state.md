# Early-2006 opening state

**ID:** `design.mvp.early-2006-opening-state`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.economy.opening-conditions-and-transmission`, `design.campaign.historical-temporality`

The first playable cut is one thin live FOMC cycle opening shortly before the Chair's first 2006 policy decision and closing when the next meeting's Morning Book arrives. It is a bounded scenario, not a campaign, succession system, complete world model, or promise of every cataloged representation.

The opening profile supplies researched identity, effective offices, authority, public history, legal instruments, staff and participant roster, calendar anchors, policy stance, selected accounts and positions, exogenous release tape, external adapter state, beliefs, access, and unresolved commitments. Exogenous releases and hidden adapter values are fixed before play. Keyed variation is limited to declared attention, interpretation, and bounded market-response draws.

The policy problem is inflation persistence against cooling interest-sensitive activity, with mild Treasury intermediation pressure as a secondary channel. It must remain a genuine decision under incomplete evidence rather than a disguised crisis with one authored answer.

The frozen scenario manifest and opening bundle are authoritative. Broader regional, provider, campaign, and calibration proposals do not change this fixture until accepted content passes the existing validation and freeze workflow.
