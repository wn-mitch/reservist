# Coalitions, sovereigns, and geography

**ID:** `design.representation.coalitions-sovereigns-and-geography`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.relationships-and-affiliations`

Durable alignment remains relationship or epistemic state. An operational coalition begins only when autonomous participants coordinate around one concrete authored proposition: a package, branch, appointment, procedure, bounded crisis operation, or institutional program. It records contributions, promises, demands, performance, and exit. It owns no mind, legal authority, commands, execution, or conserved stock.

A `SovereignSystem` is an identity scope and scenario composition root, not an Agent or owner class. Officeholders, cabinets, ministries, legislatures, courts where modeled, central banks, militaries, sovereign funds, state firms, institutions, coalitions, Pops, and firms own cognition, authority, capabilities, accounts, and actions. A `FederatedSystem` similarly composes institutions bound by a shared mandate they cannot individually opt out of.

Regions scope geography and typed mappings. They do not own stocks merely because something occurs within them. Sovereign borders, currency areas, waters, trade routes, climate systems, media spheres, and operational regions may overlap through typed scope relationships. Overlap grants no containment, authority, title, or action by default.

External subjects are promoted only when their distinct action ownership materially changes a receiving channel. Otherwise a typed aggregate or boundary provider supplies the declared interface.
