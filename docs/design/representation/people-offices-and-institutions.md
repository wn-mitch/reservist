# People, offices, and institutions

**ID:** `design.representation.people-offices-and-institutions`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.representation-axes`

Named people are rare, persistent identities whose individual discretion materially changes a modeled channel. They own private cognition, memory, relationships, dispositions, and attributable actions. Their Pop memberships may shape priors and presentation, while aggregate person cells and households retain demographic and material quantities unless an explicit personal account is independently causal and reconciled against a residual.

An office is a persistent role that grants dated duties, access, action domains, and authority only through effective law or delegation. Officeholding links a person to that role. Succession changes the holder without transferring private cognition or personal relationships.

Institutions durably own accounts, staff, facilities, records, commitments, procedures, constraints, reputation, and execution state. They have no singular humanlike belief. People deliberate, staff products synthesize, decision bodies authorize, and responsible systems execute.

Default to one institution with internal composition. Promote a desk, unit, committee, facility, subsidiary, productive site, or legal vehicle only when it independently owns a consequential queue, account, authority, access boundary, commitment, condition, transformation, or execution result. Promotion gives stable identity but does not imply separate legal or accounting ownership; those owners remain explicit.
