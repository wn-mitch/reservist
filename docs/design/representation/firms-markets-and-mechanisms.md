# Firms, markets, and mechanisms

**ID:** `design.representation.firms-markets-and-mechanisms`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.representation-axes`

A named firm receives explicit accounts, facilities, capacity, financing, contracts, governance, beliefs, and actions only when its distinct behavior materially changes a channel. Industry and organization cohorts represent similar legal persons through typed distributions. Corporate groups, subsidiaries, and joint ventures remain separate owners when their charters, liabilities, control rights, contracts, or action sets differ; consolidated views are derived.

A producing firm or cohort owns rights, dated operating plans, available and
committed capacity, input contracts, employment, finance, output accounts, and
supply offers. A productive mechanism owns scheduled work, equipment condition,
maintenance and outage queues, input consumption, witnessed yield or loss, and
release timing. Storage, pipeline, terminal, vessel, treatment, generation, and
distribution mechanisms remain distinct when their queues or failures change an
outcome.

Markets accept orders or demand schedules from participants and determine endogenous prices, quantities, fills, and residuals under declared constraints. Markets do not deliberate. A mechanical system owns administered queue processing, deterministic transformations, infrastructure condition, release calendars, or other rule-bound execution. Productive sites use mechanical-system identity when their capacity, queue, condition, transformation, outage, or result is causal.

A financial facility is a terms sheet plus authorization, eligibility, capacity, take-up, and wind-down ledgers. It is distinct from a productive site and from a market. A published rate or index is a reference, not the mechanism that measures or publishes it.

Participants retain cognition and constraints; institutions retain accounts and authority; markets and mechanisms retain their own state and witnesses. No derived market or repo view becomes a competing owner.
