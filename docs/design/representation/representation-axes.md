# Representation axes

**ID:** `design.representation.representation-axes`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.state.canonical-state-and-ownership`

Every represented subject has one stable catalog identity and one identity clade. Canonical ownership, cognition permission, authority, scenario resolution, selected fidelity, presentation salience, and player visibility remain separate declarations. No axis may infer another: a named object need not own state, an owner need not deliberate, an institution need not have one mind, and a visible subject need not receive richer causal resolution.

Identity clades define instance-level causal purposes and required state. Closed kinds include people, offices, decision bodies, staff units, institutions, organization and industry cohorts, named firms, populations and households, non-owning lenses, coalitions, sovereign and federated composition roots, regions, markets, mechanical systems, facilities, legal instruments, published references, schedules, records, outlets, networks, stateful external processes, and generators.

Instrument families and physical product families are type-level vocabularies referenced by positions, accounts, inventories, contracts, transformations, and transmissions. They are not represented subjects and own nothing.

New kinds require a demonstrated ontology failure after testing existing kinds, typed relationships, and promotable subobjects. A convenient organizational noun, UI grouping, or missing catalog row is not a new kind.
