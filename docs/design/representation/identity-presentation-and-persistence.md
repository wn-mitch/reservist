# Identity presentation and persistence

**ID:** `design.representation.identity-presentation-and-persistence`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.representation-axes`, `design.state.persistence-and-identity`

Stable historical identity, institutional continuity, officeholding, succession, legal temporality, and effective relationships are canonical. Runtime systems reason over stable IDs and dated records rather than display labels. Mergers, reorganizations, renamed subjects, appointments, and legal succession are explicit transitions, not aliases.

Presentation owns species, portrait, costume, voice, localization, display names, and satirical rendering. A presentation change never replaces a person or institution, grants a trait, alters behavior, or changes replay identity. Species cannot encode nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, ideology, intelligence, trustworthiness, moral worth, or other protected human identity. Pointed authored caricature requires contextual review and counterweight; categorical vermin mappings are excluded.

Every selected representation persists all behavior-affecting state required by its fidelity contract. Named people retain private cognition and relationships; offices retain duties and institutional records; institutions retain accounts and procedures; populations retain conserved cells and households; processes retain evolving condition; queues and witnesses retain deterministic order. Derived projections may rebuild exactly. Approximate reconstruction of omitted canonical state is forbidden.

Presentation registers remain separate from the manifest hash so localization and art iteration cannot alter simulation identity.
