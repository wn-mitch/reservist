# Authored options and previews

**ID:** `design.interface.authored-options-and-previews`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.interface.speaking-and-commitment`, `design.information.retrospective-conclusions`

Conversation choices use complete reviewed lines, not runtime generation or sentence fragments. Each option binds to reusable typed actions, claims, commitments, eligibility rules, and context predicates. Godot renders the authored line and submits its stable option ID; Rust resolves its current binding.

Every effectful option provides a practical card. The exact section names the command or communication act, current authority, recipients, timing, immediate commitments, disclosures, capacity cost, and explicit non-effects. The assessment section presents institutionally sourced expectations, uncertainty, confidence, disagreement, missing evidence, and contingent risks. It never reveals canonical future outcomes or hidden action success.

Preview is pure. Opening, hovering, comparing, or combining cards does not reserve resources, consume capacity, advance time, submit commands, draw randomness, or alter an open folder. The combined slate preview calculates immediate compatibility and aggregate capacity from the folder's settled context.

Authored options may vary with legitimate player knowledge and context. They cannot omit a feasible deadline response to force a refusal, or present a mechanically impossible choice as available. Offline authoring assistance remains subject to ordinary review and content validation.
