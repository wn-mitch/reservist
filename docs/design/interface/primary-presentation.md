# Primary presentation

**ID:** `design.interface.primary-presentation`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.information.evidence-routing`, `design.product.player-role`

Decision packets, sequenced slide decks, bounded conversations, calls, interviews, and first-person institutional vignettes are the primary surfaces. Each consequential decision receives a finite complete packet rather than equal weight on a global dashboard. Packets foreground required choices, material changes, staff recommendations and dissent, affected commitments, evidence gaps, deadlines, and inspectable source records.

Progressive depth runs from headline to brief, assessment and dissent, source record, then provenance and revision history. Slide decks present an institutional argument and evidence sequence, not unrelated metric cards. Specialist views open from a relevant artifact, prepared scene, or knowledgeable participant and retain a link to that source context.

The building is a set of direct scene shortcuts and visual-novel vignettes, not a traversal simulation. A scene exposes only brought materials, people present, available records, and authorized retrieval paths. Presentation resolution may foreground any initialized subject without changing causal fidelity.

Routing controls attention, access controls what can be known, and the archive preserves every delivered artifact. There is no universal numeric inbox cap; decision-centered bundling, competent routing, and optional depth control load.
