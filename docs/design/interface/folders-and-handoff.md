# Folders and handoff

**ID:** `design.interface.folders-and-handoff`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.institutions.policy-portfolios`, `design.state.correction-and-invalidation`

Each meeting, decision, call, or interview has a working folder containing its settled context, source artifacts, available authored choices, penciled selections, practical cards, combined constraints, and expected handoff recipient. While the folder is open, ordinary pencils are provisional and may be revised without mutating canonical state.

The folder shows both each choice's exact card and the combined slate. Admission validates authority prerequisites, incompatibilities, aggregate capacity, deadlines, and other immediate constraints together. Closing and handing off commits the complete valid slate atomically. A rejected slate commits none of its pencils. Admission does not guarantee authorization by another body, execution, take-up, market clearing, settlement, or downstream outcome.

After handoff, the folder, selected commands, receipt, and witnesses are immutable history. Later cancellation, correction, amendment, or reversal uses the affected instrument's real lifecycle and creates linked records. Closing an unsubmitted folder discards only provisional pencils; it cannot revoke a speech act or inquiry that already committed.

Navigation, inspection, filtering, comparison, and other non-effectful UI actions remain immediate. Extra confirmation layers are reserved for genuinely unusual destructive consequences.
