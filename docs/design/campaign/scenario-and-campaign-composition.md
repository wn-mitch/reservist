# Scenario and campaign composition

**ID:** `design.campaign.scenario-and-campaign-composition`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.campaign.historical-temporality`, `design.runtime.scenario-selection`

Campaigns compose independently meaningful Chair, doctrine, organization, and scenario presets. The Chair preset supplies identity, office eligibility, initial private cognition, relationships, public claims, and program options. Doctrine shapes priors and declared policy posture. Organization initializes staff, capacities, procedures, records, and institutional condition. Scenario supplies historical regime, law, roster, opening state, schedules, hazards, and external boundaries.

Preset composition cannot grant authority, knowledge, capability, or operational access absent from the selected period and institution. Counterfactual appointments remain constrained by effective law, staff, relationships, and operational knowledge. Historical roleplay uses researched combinations; controlled doctrine experiments may offer compatible alternatives.

A scenario generator may select from reviewed predefined content before a run. Once initialized, the causal roster, owners, fidelity, and fallbacks remain fixed. Prevention may genuinely avert a familiar crisis; the simulation does not conserve misery or force replacement drama.

Campaign state carries institutional records, commitments, proceedings, facilities, capacity, legal state, and score history across scenario and Chair boundaries. Each scenario retains its own frozen content identity and provenance. Campaign composition cannot silently rewrite a completed scenario.
