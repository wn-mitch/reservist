# Review and finalization

**ID:** `design.campaign.review-and-finalization`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.campaign.stewardship-score`, `design.campaign.chair-succession`

A completed institutional review is first an in-world record with evidence, confidence, dissent, timing, and access boundaries. Dispositioning it may finalize applicable authored Stewardship findings. The player may accept, attach a Chair response, request a specific evidence-backed revision, or accept while commissioning a supplemental review. Disposition does not imply agreement.

An adverse valid score cannot be suppressed by refusing closure. Returning a report or commissioning more work consumes concrete capacity and may delay other obligations. Any resulting harm is evaluated through witnessed displacement, obstruction, missed deadlines, or degraded outcomes, never a recursive penalty for disputing the score.

Each chairmanship closes with a dossier preserving mandate outcomes, crisis performance, institutional capacity, legal and procedural integrity, future optionality, inherited conditions, incurred costs, public commitments, and outstanding obligations. Succession continues the campaign with this state. The final bounded campaign endpoint produces an institutional dossier across all chairs and the current immutable score ledger.

The terminal rule is explicit: after the final review and any required disposition path, the campaign records one final state and stops accepting gameplay commands. Saves and replays preserve that terminal state; refusal to open a review cannot keep the campaign indefinitely unfinalized.
