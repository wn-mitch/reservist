# Chairmanship program

**ID:** `design.campaign.chairmanship-program`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.institutions.policy-portfolios`, `design.campaign.scenario-and-campaign-composition`

Every Chair begins with a populated `ChairmanshipProgram` record rather than a blank slate. The Chair preset supplies one primary aspiration, up to two supporting aspirations, inherited institutional projects, prior public claims, relevant relationships, and commitments already attached to the office or person. Historical roleplay uses the researched preset; compatible setup modes may adjust declared choices.

The program gives legible term-long direction without granting bonuses, revealing hidden truth, or becoming a causal completion meter. Aspirations organize proposals, projects, monitoring, and later review. They do not authorize actions or override effective law, institutional procedure, staff capacity, market response, or external conditions.

Progress consists of witnessed work and consequences linked to ordinary records, commands, commitments, and outcomes. A program can be revised prospectively through an explicit record transition. Earlier claims, abandoned plans, incurred costs, and completed work remain history.

The program is not the Stewardship score. Pursuing or completing an aspiration earns nothing automatically. Evaluation considers lawful execution, proportionality, outcomes, preparedness, exit, costs, and the institutional condition handed onward. Unfinished or inherited work remains visible at succession.
