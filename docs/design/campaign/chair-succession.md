# Chair succession

**ID:** `design.campaign.chair-succession`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.representation.people-offices-and-institutions`, `design.institutions.failure-resolution-and-continuity`

Campaign play continues through every valid Chair succession until the campaign's bounded endpoint. Resignation, incapacity, death, removal, failed renomination, ordinary term expiry, and statutory reorganization enter the same legal succession framework. Effective law and appointment procedure select the incoming Chair; player control then binds to that person.

The office and institution continue. Official records, facilities, staff, legal state, public commitments, unresolved proceedings, monitoring obligations, operational capacity, credibility consequences, and inherited conditions persist. Private beliefs, personal memory, person-owned commitments, dispositions, and personal relationships remain with the outgoing person and do not transfer.

Each chairmanship receives a distinct dossier. Succession records inherited state, the outgoing handoff, effective officeholding changes, and the incoming information set. It cannot become a soft reset for damaged legitimacy, independence, capability, trust, or outstanding obligations.

Removal is a canonical legal and political event, not a score threshold and not automatically a score deduction. The campaign evaluates witnessed stewardship facts surrounding it. Only the campaign terminal rule ends play; loss of one officeholder alone does not switch the player to an omniscient institution or terminate the world.
