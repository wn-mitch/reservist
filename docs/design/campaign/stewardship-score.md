# Stewardship score

**ID:** `design.campaign.stewardship-score`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `design.campaign.chairmanship-program`, `design.information.retrospective-conclusions`

Stewardship is one explicit unbounded signed campaign total answering whether the player left the office capable, lawful, credible in its commitments, and able to meet future shocks and obligations. It is extradiegetic, non-spendable, non-causal, and invisible to world actors. It is not reputation, credibility, legitimacy, independence, authority, or a world-state variable.

Awards and deductions require explicit authored findings satisfied by witnessed accomplishments, failures, costs, proportionality, legality, exit, preparation, and handoff condition. Activity and action names earn nothing. Severe later failures may erase earlier gains; duration creates opportunities but is not normalized away. Removal itself earns no automatic deduction.

Standing criteria and point values are visible from the beginning. Contingent criteria appear when their subject becomes institutionally known and never reveal undiscovered facts. One global denomination and magnitude rubric applies across scenarios; scenarios select applicable findings and contextual magnitude but cannot invent incompatible scoring economies.

Entries form an immutable ledger keyed to an accepted review and version. Later evidence creates upward or downward compensating entries. It never silently rewrites prior deltas. Exact magnitude bands remain balancing work against representative postmortems.
