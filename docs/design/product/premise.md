# Product premise

**ID:** `design.product.premise`
**Status:** `canonical`
**Depends on:** `none`

Reservist is a turn-based, satirical institutional crisis simulator centered on the Federal Reserve Chair's office. The player operates one bounded node inside markets, public institutions, political systems, households, firms, media, and external shocks. The simulation contains canonical underlying state, but the player encounters it only through institutionally produced evidence and fallible interpretation.

Difficulty comes from judgment under partial information, competing obligations, delayed effects, and finite institutional capacity. It never comes from player wall-clock pressure. Decisions must matter through owned causal mechanisms rather than scripted outcome flags. Historical plausibility constrains opening conditions, authorities, available instruments, and mechanism direction; it does not force historical outcomes.

Satire belongs to presentation. It may target people, institutions, brands, dynasties, or conduct through reviewed authored treatment, but it never changes causal identity or encodes protected human traits as animal character. Technical explanation may teach mechanisms without revealing hidden run state.

The product supports continued institutional stewardship across Chair successions. The world does not become an omniscient control panel, a macroeconomic spreadsheet, a universal-agent simulation, or a fixed visual novel.
