# Resource truth and estimates proposal

**ID:** `proposal.world.resource-truth-and-estimates`
**Status:** `proposal`
**Depends on:** `proposal.world.arabian-reference-chain`
**Proposes changes to:** `design.economy.opening-conditions-and-transmission`, `design.state.canonical-state-and-ownership`

This proposal is nonbinding. It makes no catalog entry or scenario ready.

A regional resource process would own latent recoverable physical condition: accessibility, depletion, recovery, discovery, heat, aquifer, and other unowned reservoir state. An accepted extraction action would enter through a typed process input, record before-and-after depletion, and only then release a balanced quantity into a legal right holder's titled inventory. A producer could not create oil, gas, or water by revising an estimate.

Producer geological estimates and booked reserves would remain separate belief and accounting state. Each would name measurement source, method, valuation policy, uncertainty, observation time, and owner. A survey or reestimate could change those values without changing latent physical truth. A discovery transition could alter latent state without automatically informing a producer or changing its books.

Water would require stage-specific non-fungibility: source, raw or extracted, potable treated, industrial process, and reclaimed or waste stages. Each stage would declare conserved unit, quality, contamination and salinity, storage and loss, custody, treatment, distribution, delivery eligibility, and valid uses. Transformation and delivery witnesses would be required before one stage satisfies another's obligation.
