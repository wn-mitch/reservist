# World atlas scope proposal

**ID:** `proposal.world.atlas-scope`
**Status:** `proposal`
**Depends on:** `none`
**Proposes changes to:** `design.content.regional-promotion`

This proposal is nonbinding. It makes no catalog entry or scenario ready.

The regional research program would maintain a broad cross-era planning atlas rather than begin with a fixed five-region set. Candidate lanes include Gulf energy and water; petroleum and LNG chokepoints; Amazon and Congo forest, agriculture, hydrology, and access; African, Andean, Australian, and Indonesian mineral corridors; Black Sea and American grain systems; North American energy; Southeast and Northeast Asian manufacturing, shipping, power, and LNG; and semiconductor logistics around the Taiwan Strait.

Each atlas row would name material or capacity lanes and the distinct causal reason to retain that scope. Rows would remain reading and research indexes, not simulation state, fidelity commitments, present-day defaults, or promises of scenario inclusion. Country, watershed, coastal, industrial, transport, climate, currency, and media scopes could overlap without implying ownership.

Period-specific owners, rights, law, capacity, inventories, accounts, counterparties, opening state, and calibration would remain scenario content. Acceptance would add only the roster discipline to `doc:design.content.regional-promotion`; concrete rows would still require ordinary catalog authoring and validation.
