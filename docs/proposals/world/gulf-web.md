# Gulf web

**ID:** `index.world.gulf-web`
**Status:** `index`
**Depends on:** `proposal.world.regional-composition-boundary`, `proposal.world.commodity-production-model`, `proposal.world.national-resource-management`, `proposal.world.authored-strategic-interpretation`, `proposal.world.observations-and-fed-transmission`, `proposal.world.cross-era-middle-east-scenarios`

This index is the integration spine for the Gulf reference world. It owns no
independent rule and makes no catalog entry or scenario ready. Its purpose is to
show how the split proposal leaves compose one causal web.

The reference world asks how different political formulations of the Middle
East produce different economic realities. Its governing proposition is that
resource power is not endowment. It is the ability to convert an endowment into
delivered, paid, and politically usable flow:

`resource -> extraction -> transformation -> storage -> route -> delivery ->`
`settlement -> fiscal and financial power`

The [regional boundary](regional-composition-boundary.md) defines the Gulf
countries, overlapping geographic scopes, durable institutions, temporary
formations, and the rule of universal representation with unequal resolution.
The [production model](commodity-production-model.md) supplies the common
stock, transformation, capacity, inventory, transport, contract, and demand
grammar. [Petroleum and gas](petroleum-gas-system.md), [logistics and
chokepoints](logistics-chokepoints.md), and the [water, power, and industrial
nexus](water-power-industrial-nexus.md) specialize that grammar for crude
grades, gas and LNG, products and naphtha, petrochemicals, fertilizer, water,
electricity, shipping, and alternate routes.

[National resource management](national-resource-management.md) separates
government policy from operator execution and physical feasibility. The
[strategic-interpretation model](authored-strategic-interpretation.md) gives
sovereigns bounded theory of mind while leaving wars, revolutions, embargoes,
regime transitions, and novel escalation authored. Sunni authority, Shia
authority, and Arab-Palestinian legitimacy remain independent regional forces,
not national opinion scores or complete political simulations.

Contracts create claims rather than guaranteed delivery. Political eligibility,
physical compatibility, committed allocation, discretionary breach, residual
markets, and unmet demand remain distinct stages. Corporate and household
cohorts participate without becoming thousands of individually simulated
agents. Infrastructure develops inside authored feasible-project envelopes;
actors may finance, resize, delay, accelerate, mothball, or cancel reachable
projects.

The [fiscal and financial chain](fiscal-monetary-portfolio-transmission.md)
connects cargoes to payment currencies, trade credit, public receipts, reserves,
banks, sovereign portfolios, and U.S. asset demand. [Observation and Fed
transmission](observations-and-fed-transmission.md) delivers the world through
continuous period media, diplomatic and intelligence reporting, markets, staff
analysis, annotations, and institutional follow-up rather than canonical truth.
The [scenario family](cross-era-middle-east-scenarios.md) applies the same web
to the 1970s oil shocks, Desert Storm, and a contemporary Hormuz crisis.

The runtime uses canonical UTC event time, local institutional calendars and
market sessions, daily settlement, and ordered intraday events. Every country
remains legible in a lower-fidelity world model; selected owners and mechanisms
are promoted where their independent behavior changes the receiving scenario.
Future playable institutions should reuse these contracts, but playable
compatibility is not a readiness gate.
