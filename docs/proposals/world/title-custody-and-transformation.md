# Title, custody, and transformation proposal

**ID:** `proposal.world.title-custody-and-transformation`
**Status:** `proposal`
**Depends on:** `proposal.world.ownership-and-interfaces`
**Proposes changes to:** `design.economy.accounting-and-settlement`, `design.content.instrument-and-product-families`

This proposal is nonbinding. It makes no catalog entry or scenario ready.

Transformation and transport would be permitted to change physical stage, quantity through witnessed yield or loss, custody, location, quality, and queue status without changing legal title. Title would move only at an agreement-defined transfer point, through a clearing allocation followed by settlement, or through another explicit legal or accounting transition with authority and witness.

The invariant would apply to extraction release, storage, pipeline receipt, terminal intake, free-on-board loading, tanker handoff, delivered delivery, regasification, refinery intake, treatment, and later product stages. A carrier, terminal, warehouse, refinery, or transformation mechanism would not acquire inventory merely by accepting custody.

Every promoted provider would reconcile the prior external quantity into explicit promoted source accounts plus remaining residual source accounts. Reconciliation would be independent by product, grade or eligibility, title-bearing account scope, and delivery window. Before endogenous response is enabled, fallback would reproduce the replaced adapter's declared outputs, units, timing, provenance, and residual totals.

Acceptance would refine canonical title and family contracts; actual transfer points, Incoterms, rights, accounts, and period law would still require source-backed catalog and scenario content.
