# Regional commodity production proposal

**ID:** `proposal.world.commodity-production-model`
**Status:** `proposal`
**Depends on:** `proposal.world.arabian-reference-chain`, `proposal.world.resource-truth-and-estimates`, `proposal.world.ownership-and-interfaces`
**Proposes changes to:** `design.economy.real-economy-network`, `design.content.regional-promotion`, `design.representation.firms-markets-and-mechanisms`

This proposal is nonbinding. It makes no catalog entry or scenario ready.

Regional production would be composed from a producer decision layer and a
physical execution layer. A promoted firm, productive site, or industry cohort
would own productive rights, available and committed capacity, input contracts,
employment, finance, operating plans, output accounts, and supply offers. A
mechanical extraction or conversion system would own scheduled work, equipment
condition, maintenance and outage queues, input consumption, witnessed yield or
loss, and release timing. A resource process would retain latent reservoir or
environmental condition under `doc:proposal.world.resource-truth-and-estimates`.

Each production attempt would pass through an explicit stage sequence:

1. the right holder proposes a dated quantity and commits producer-controlled
   capacity, finance, labor, and contracted inputs;
2. effective law, rights, quotas, contracts, and delivery eligibility authorize,
   limit, redirect, or reject the attempt;
3. the operating mechanism admits work subject to available equipment,
   maintenance, storage, transport, power, water, and other typed constraints;
4. extraction consumes recoverable physical state, while conversion consumes
   titled input inventory;
5. execution records joint outputs, grade, losses, emissions or waste where
   modeled, custody, and provenance;
6. accepted output enters the right holder's titled account before any transport,
   sale, benchmark publication, or downstream price effect.

Capacity would not be one scalar. The minimum common ledger would distinguish
nameplate, technically available, legally available, input-supported, committed,
and realized throughput. Commodity-specific extensions would define decline or
recovery, biological or harvest timing, co-products, grade distributions,
restart delays, substitution, and storage constraints. These extensions would
share stage contracts without forcing oil wells, mines, farms, and factories
through one response equation.

Promotion would select the lowest aggregation at which an attributable choice
or independently varying constraint changes the receiving scenario. A named
site would replace its exact share of a producer cohort; a named producer or
country allocation would replace its exact share of the regional provider; all
unpromoted production would remain in reconciled residual accounts. Site,
producer, country, basin, and regional observations could disagree because they
are measured and revised objects rather than privileged views of canonical
state.

The Arabian reference chain would first test four difficult distinctions:
producer targets versus realized extraction; crude and associated-gas joint
output; domestic processing or water-and-power demand versus export allocation;
and spare capacity versus capacity that cannot arrive inside the scenario's
decision horizon. Period content would supply the actual rights, operators,
quotas, costs, maintenance state, infrastructure, accounts, observations, and
calibration.

The common commodity grammar would cover resource or input stocks,
transformation recipes, productive capacity, inventories, transport, contracts,
payment, and final demand. Household cohorts would consume final goods;
industry cohorts would transform, store, or consume intermediate goods; and
governments could procure, subsidize, distribute, or stockpile through the same
conserved substrate. Political eligibility and physical feasibility would
precede committed claims, permitted diversion or breach, residual spot clearing,
and recorded unmet demand.

Most refiners, traders, carriers, insurers, banks, and buyers would remain typed
corporate cohorts with distributions of technology, jurisdiction, contracts,
finance, and risk acceptance. Named firms would be promoted only for unique
assets, relationships, or systemic consequences. A lower-fidelity world model
would supply competing producers, importer demand, inventories, substitution,
and benchmark formation while the Gulf and United States receive richer
resolution. This is universal representation with unequal detail, not an
exogenous world-price oracle.
