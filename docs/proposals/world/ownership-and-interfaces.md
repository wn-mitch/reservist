# Arabian ownership and interfaces proposal

**ID:** `proposal.world.ownership-and-interfaces`
**Status:** `proposal`
**Depends on:** `proposal.world.arabian-reference-chain`, `proposal.world.resource-truth-and-estimates`
**Proposes changes to:** `design.state.canonical-state-and-ownership`, `design.economy.external-provider-contracts`, `design.economy.accounting-and-settlement`

This proposal is nonbinding. It makes no catalog entry or scenario ready.

The region would own only scope version, overlaps, jurisdiction references, and scoped-subject references. A resource process would own declared unowned physical conditions and recovery history. Legal right holders and producers would own rights, productive capacity, extracted inventory, contracts, employment, financing, and supply offers. Extraction, refinery, liquefaction, desalination, storage, pipeline, terminal, tanker, and export mechanisms would own queues, operating condition, transformation, custody references, loss, and release schedules.

Physical markets would own eligibility, orders, clearing, allocation, rationing, and failures. Benchmark publishers would own methodology, calendars, vintages, revisions, and published records, not market clearing. A selected futures market and clearing system would separately own contracts, positions, margins, defaults, and settlement or delivery procedures, never producer inventory.

Each output would name product and grade, origin, title-bearing account, delivery window, contract, unit, timing, provenance, and uncertainty. The existing Gulf aggregate could remain a lower-fidelity U.S.-facing energy provider. Rich Arabian owners would replace a declared share and independently reconcile the remainder rather than layering over it.
