# Paul Vulture character bible

Presentation for `person.us.paul_vulture`. Bespoke tier under the art-direction
rule (`design.interface.art-direction-and-character-production`). The approved
portrait is the one registered in `catalog/presentation_refs.csv`; the other
files in this folder are earlier or alternate generations. Prompt history is in
`../PROMPTS.txt` (rounds 3, 5, and 6).

## Invariants

Every portrait, cutout, scene, and event illustration keeps:

- a very tall overall presence with an elderly, stooped posture;
- a complete, dignified vulture head with species-native plumage and face
  planes, charcoal and pale-gray crown feathers, and no human scalp or skin;
- calm, deep-set avian eyes and heavy brows expressed as feather ridges;
- a small rounded hooked beak proportionate to the face;
- a long, narrow neck with little or no projecting feather ruff;
- charcoal neck feathers ending cleanly above a conventional shirt collar;
- the suit, not the plumage, defining the shoulder silhouette;
- an oversized or rumpled dark suit, off-white shirt, and subdued dark rust tie;
- large dark glasses;
- one restrained short, thick cigar at the right corner of the beak, with a
  small dull ember and at most a faint wisp of smoke;
- an intelligent, tired, grave, humane demeanor.

## Known failure modes

- Frilly white feather ruff, scarf-like neck plumage, or an oversized collar
  effect. Corrected in round 5 by restating plain suit, modest collar, and a
  clean feather-to-collar line.
- Predatory, skeletal, angry, or horror-like rendering. Counter by stating
  "intelligent, tired, and humane" explicitly.
- Bald human scalp or human facial skin on a vulture head.
