# Jimmy Darter character bible

Presentation for the President in the Volcker lens, an anthropomorphic
greyhound interpretation of Jimmy Carter. Bespoke tier under
`design.interface.art-direction-and-character-production`; species rules are in
`../../species/greyhound.md`. Cast names are office labels in the simulation
until a catalog person is authored; this bible defines presentation only.

## Invariants

Every portrait, cutout, scene, and event illustration keeps:

- a greyhound head with a sandy, light-brown coat graying at the muzzle and a
  groomed, side-swept crown marking in place of Carter's hair;
- light, direct eyes and an earnest, closed-mouth expression; the direction is
  serious and unsmiling, so the famous broad grin is never drawn;
- a slight, upright build of a man in his mid-fifties;
- a conservative 1979 dark or mid-gray suit, white shirt, and narrow dark tie;
  a beige cardigan over shirt and tie replaces the jacket only in fireside or
  residence scenes;
- a composed, tired, principled demeanor.
