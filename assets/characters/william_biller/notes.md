# William Biller supporting notes

Presentation for the Secretary of the Treasury in the Volcker lens, an
anthropomorphic duck interpretation of G. William Miller. Supporting tier under
`design.interface.art-direction-and-character-production`; species rules are in
`../../species/duck.md`.

- **Age and build:** a man in his mid-fifties, compact and square-shouldered.
- **Identity cues:** receding dark head plumage combed back; a reserved,
  businesslike expression; a precise, upright posture from naval service; a
  steady gaze of a corporate executive turned official.
- **Wardrobe:** dark conservative 1979 suit, white shirt, subdued tie; no
  pocket square or jewelry.
