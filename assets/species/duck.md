# Duck species guide

Species guide under `design.interface.art-direction-and-character-production`.
Used by William Biller (supporting; see `../characters/william_biller/notes.md`).

- **Head and body:** a rounded duck head with a flat, broad bill, eyes high on
  the head, and a compact skull. The body is humanoid below the collar and
  slightly fuller through the chest.
- **Neck to clothing:** a short neck; head feathers end at the collar without a
  visible feather collar or ruff.
- **Feather treatment:** smooth, close head plumage with subtle species
  markings. Historical hair becomes plumage tone or a crown marking.
- **Hands:** humanoid hands; no webbed feet or wing-hands in clothed scenes.
- **Clothed silhouette:** compact and square-shouldered in a conservative suit.
- **Observed failures:** none yet; no duck generation has been reviewed. Watch
  for a cartoon or comic duck reading, a smiling bill, and bright plumage that
  breaks the muted period palette.
