# Greyhound species guide

Species guide under `design.interface.art-direction-and-character-production`.
Used by Jimmy Darter (bespoke; see `../characters/jimmy_darter/notes.md`).

- **Head and body:** a long, narrow greyhound skull with a fine muzzle, a
  shallow stop, and folded rose ears carried back. Eyes are set at the sides of
  a slender face. The body is lean and humanoid below the collar.
- **Neck to clothing:** a long, clean neck with a short smooth coat meeting the
  collar without fluff or mane.
- **Coat treatment:** short, close, smooth coat. Historical hair becomes coat
  color and a groomed marking on the crown, never a human hairpiece.
- **Hands:** slim humanoid hands with a short coat on the backs; no paws in
  clothed scenes.
- **Clothed silhouette:** slight and upright, narrow shoulders, suit cut close.
- **Observed failures:** none yet; no greyhound generation has been reviewed.
  Watch for a human face on a dog head, a cartoon grin, and a heavy or broad
  hound build that loses the greyhound line.
