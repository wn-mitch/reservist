# Vulture species guide

Species guide under `design.interface.art-direction-and-character-production`.
Used by Paul Vulture (bespoke; see `../characters/paul_vulture/notes.md`).

- **Head and body:** a complete vulture skull and face planes: a long, narrow
  head, a small rounded hooked beak, deep-set eyes under feathered brow ridges.
  The head sits on a long, narrow neck; the body below the collar is humanoid.
- **Neck to clothing:** neck feathers end cleanly above a conventional shirt
  collar. No projecting ruff; the suit defines the shoulder line.
- **Feather treatment:** short, dense, species-native crown and neck plumage in
  charcoal and pale gray. Historical hair becomes plumage, never a human scalp.
- **Hands:** humanoid hands with a faint scaled or leathery texture; no talons
  in clothed scenes.
- **Clothed silhouette:** tall and narrow, slightly stooped; jacket hangs loose.
- **Observed failures:** frilly white ruff or scarf-like plumage, predatory or
  skeletal rendering, and a bald human scalp on the head.
